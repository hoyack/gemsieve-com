# Review handoff

## CMO

Confirm locked title/H1, filter-intelligence positioning, original visual direction, CTA hierarchy, screenshots, and absence of jewelry framing or fake customer proof.

## CRO

Confirm ICP, early-access offer, qualified pilot event, revenue hypothesis, analytics funnel, and 30/60/90 plan in `docs/CRO.md`.

## CTO

Review static architecture, dependency audit, CSP, forms, CI, and Netlify runbook. CTO owns approval/merge, production linkage, DNS snapshot and preservation, HTTPS, analytics activation, monitoring, form-delivery proof, and rollback evidence.

## Closure evidence

Open approved PR; green CI; preview and desktop/mobile screenshots; CMO/CRO approvals; CTO production URL; HTTPS and route tests; real Netlify form receipt; analytics configuration; monitoring; DNS snapshot; rollback record; content owner and cadence.
