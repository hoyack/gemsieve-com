# Continuous content loop

## Owner and cadence

Product marketing owns a monthly “Sieve Note” and quarterly use-case refresh. Product/CRO review claims and CTA. Legal/privacy review any new data category. CTO reviews code, CSP, scripts, and deployment.

## Source lanes

Use consented interviews, pilot observations approved for publication, public primary sources, and internal product changelogs. Record URL or interview ID, access date, owner, claim supported, and permission status.

## Template

Decision → noisy input field → explicit hard gates → scored signals → evidence standard → uncertainty → shortlist → what changed after review. Never publish customer names, data, or outcomes without written permission.

## Freshness metrics

Monthly: source age, broken links, CTA/form delivery, policy accuracy, and copy-to-product drift. Quarterly: use-case evidence, product-stage statements, stale screenshots, conversion trend, and pages with no owner review in 90 days.
