Privacy Notice

How Hoyack handles information submitted through GemSieve forms.

Last updated: July 12, 2026.

What we collect

Waitlist and pilot forms may collect your name, work email, organization, workflow selection, and the details you choose to submit.

Why we use it

We use submitted information to respond to requests, operate the waitlist, evaluate pilot fit, prevent spam, and understand aggregate demand. Our basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in operating this service.

Processors and retention

Netlify may process form submissions on our behalf. If analytics is activated, it will be configured for aggregate, privacy-minded measurement. We retain form records only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal obligations, or abuse prevention.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from updates at any time. To request access, correction, or deletion, email privacy@hoyack.com. We do not sell submitted personal information.

Changes

Material changes will be dated on this page. GemSieve is operated by Hoyack.
