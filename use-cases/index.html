Use cases

Choose the signal before the pile arrives

Reusable criteria help teams resist recency, charisma, and spreadsheet drift.

R / 01

Research triage

Input: papers, reports, interviews, search results.

Sieve: source quality, relevance, method fit, freshness, contradiction.

Output: a reading queue with reasons and citation-ready evidence.

D / 02

Deal flow

Input: company profiles, decks, referrals, data-room notes.

Sieve: thesis gates, proof strength, timing, risk, next-step value.

Output: a review slate that shows why each company earned attention.

V / 03

Vendor selection

Input: proposals, security answers, demos, references.

Sieve: must-haves, implementation fit, total cost, evidence, uncertainty.

Output: a defensible shortlist before demos distort the requirements.

C / 04

Content pipeline

Input: customer questions, search demand, pitches, internal expertise.

Sieve: audience proof, authority, distinctiveness, shelf life, distribution fit.

Output: a publish queue tied to evidence—not whoever pitched last.
