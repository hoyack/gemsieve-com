# CRO record

## ICP

Teams of 3–30 in research, investing, procurement, strategy, or content operations that review 20+ candidates repeatedly and must explain why the final few survived.

## Offer and conversion events

Top-of-funnel offer: product updates plus one practical sieve template. Primary event: `waitlist_submit`. Qualified event: `pilot_submit`. Supporting events: header, hero, and footer CTA clicks. Form fields store `source=gemsieve` and the offer (`early-access` or `private-pilot`).

## Revenue mechanism

Hypothesis 1: paid pilot to encode one workflow and validate decision value. Hypothesis 2: recurring per-team subscription for reusable sieves, evidence trails, review rounds, and exports. Pricing and commercial terms remain unclaimed.

## 30/60/90 distribution plan

- 30 days: founder-led interviews and direct outreach to 20 workflow owners; publish one sieve template.
- 60 days: run 3–5 design-partner pilots; measure completion, reason usefulness, and repeat use.
- 90 days: publish anonymized workflow learnings with permission; test partner channels and one targeted comparison page.

Guardrails: no purchased lists, fake scarcity, invented logos, outcome guarantees, or jewelry-search traffic capture.
